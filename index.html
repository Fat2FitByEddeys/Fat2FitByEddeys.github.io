PROGRAM LOCATION

Fat2Fit Hostel & Resort is tucked into the hills off the NH5 highway, on the Delhi/Chandigarh–Shimla route, pincode 171219 — just 13 km before Shimla.

Fat2Fit Resort & Villa is about 6 km up from the highway, and Fat2Fit Hostel is roughly 1 km up the hill, close to Shoghi Police Chowki and the Kawara Chowki Police Station checkpoint on NH5.
